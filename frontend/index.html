How CivicPulse Operates

A three-way interconnected loop uniting citizens, public sector officials, and skilled local technicians.

Pillar 1

Multimodal Forensic Inspection

Citizens submit photos or voice notes in Hindi, Hinglish, or English. Gemma 4 natively audits physical severity, extracts GPS landmarks, and determines statutory departmental jurisdiction.

CivicTalk Audio + Vision
Pillar 2

Statutory Legal Accountability

One-click formal grievance and RTI generator. Automatically drafts statutory notices under Municipal Corporation Acts and Section 6(1) RTI applications ready for copy or print PDF.

RTI 2005 & CrPC Notice
Pillar 3

Anti-Gouging Utility Marketplace

Defects in private residences receive AI repair benchmarks. Local verified plumbers and electricians submit transparent bids with two-way counter-offers and locked escrow payouts.

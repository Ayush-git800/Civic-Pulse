The Dual Crisis & The CivicPulse Solution

Solving municipal administrative bottlenecks and predatory home repair pricing with multimodal vision.

The Problem

  • •
    Bureaucratic Black Hole: Citizens see open electrical pits, broken roads, or sewage, but do not know which department is responsible (PWD, Municipal Corp, or Power Board).
  • •
    Jurisdictional Mismatch: Complaints filed in the wrong forum (e.g. National Highways or Railways filed with city council) get silently dropped after weeks.
  • •
    Repair Pricing Asymmetry: Homeowners have no objective way to estimate labor or replacement parts, leading to rampant price-gouging.
  • •
    Predatory Aggregator Fees: Existing gig apps charge up to 25% commissions from daily-wage technicians, inflating consumer repair bills.

The CivicPulse Solution

  • ✔
    Gemma 4 Multimodal Audit: Upload a single photo; Gemma 4 instantly determines severity, municipal department, and actionable remediation steps.
  • ✔
    Autonomous Escalation Router: Automatically identifies central vs local jurisdictions and generates 1-click links to CPGRAMS, NHAI, or RailMadad.
  • ✔
    AI Fair Market Price Brackets: Diagnoses broken appliances or leaks and computes realistic Indian market pricing for both parts and labor.
  • ✔
    Transparent Worker Bargaining: Technicians bid directly, residents counter-offer, and both lock fair deals with 0% platform commission.

How Gemma 4 Powers CivicPulse

Harnessing Google's open multimodal foundation model for real-world impact across India.

Zero-Shot Visual Forensics

Ingests raw JPEG/PNG byte streams to quantify hazard depth, vehicular hazard level, and concrete degradation with forensic engineer precision.

Jurisdiction Classifier

Differentiates municipal corporation duties from central statutory bodies (NHAI, Railway Board, CPGRAMS), preventing lost grievance tickets.

Fair Price Engine

Diagnoses broken hardware and calculates localized Indian market rates for plumber/electrician labor and replacement parts.

Worker-Customer Bidding

Interactive bargaining engine where local technicians submit bids, homeowners counter-offer, and both lock rates without middleman fees.

PulseBot Public Copilot

Conversational assistant answering municipal RTI filing steps, helpline directories, legal rights, and project background in real-time.

Resilient Fallback Cascade

High-availability engine: cascades automatically to Gemini 3.5/3.8 Flash upon quota or network spikes, delivering 99.9% uptime.

System Architecture & Engineering Rigor

Production-ready, zero-bloat stack engineered for resilience, speed, and mobile responsiveness.

Frontend Layer
Vanilla JS + Tailwind
  • • Zero framework bloat (no bloated node_modules bundles)
  • • Instant DOM rendering & dynamic masonry cards
  • • Glowing laser scanner multimodal effect
  • • High-resolution evidence lightbox with inspect zoom
Backend & APIs
FastAPI (Python 3.9+)
  • • Asynchronous, high-throughput Uvicorn ASGI server
  • • Strict Pydantic v2 data validation contracts
  • • Multi-part streaming for image file uploads
  • • Automated healthchecks & JSON response sanitization
AI Orchestration
Gemma 4 (26B-A4B-IT)
  • • Official Google GenAI SDK integration
  • • Direct image byte inspection via types.Part
  • • Fallback cascade to Gemini 3.5 & 3.8 Flash
  • • Fail-safe JSON extraction with regex repairs
Data Persistence
SQLite 3 (WAL Mode)
  • • Thread-safe connection pool with write mutexes
  • • Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) for concurrent read/write
  • • Full relational integrity (posts, comments, jobs, bids)
  • • Auto-seeding fixtures for instant zero-config demos
